Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026. This policy covers Sports Edge: the home page at ant56-arch.github.io and the NFL Edge, CFB Edge, MLB Edge and NBA Edge sites and Schedule page linked from it (together, "the sites"). The sites are static web pages with no accounts, sign-ups, forms, comments, payments, ads or analytics.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't ask for, receive or store personal information. The sites set no cookies and run no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't have any.

What's saved on your device

The Schedule page remembers whether you last chose the TV Guide or Cards view by saving that one setting in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can clear it at any time by clearing site data in your browser.

Services your browser contacts

To show a page, your browser loads files from the services below. Like any web server, they can see your IP address, your browser type and the page that made the request. Their own privacy policies apply, and we don't receive or control what they log.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the sites and may keep server logs, including IP addresses, for security and operations. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • jsDelivr serves the charting library on the Accuracy and Model pages. See the jsDelivr privacy policy.
  • ESPN (site.api.espn.com and a.espncdn.com) supplies live scores and team logos, which your browser requests directly. See the Disney privacy policy, which covers ESPN.
  • MLB (www.mlbstatic.com) serves team logos on MLB Edge. See the MLB privacy policy.

You can limit what these services see with your browser's privacy settings or a content blocker. Some parts of the pages may not load if you do.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Because the sites don't track you, there is nothing for these signals to switch off. We treat every visit as if they were on.

Children

The sites are meant for adults and aren't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or state privacy laws in the US may give you rights to access or delete personal information about you. We hold none, but you can contact us with any request and we'll answer it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. If we ever start collecting any information, we'll say so here before we do.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent by opening an issue on the site's GitHub page.
