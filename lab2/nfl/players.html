How to read this

The big number is the yards we project for him this week. Next to it is the rest of his projected line, and you can tap a player to see all of it. Each projection starts from his recent games, then gets adjusted for the defense he faces and for injuries. Players listed Out are left off.

Soft matchup means the defense he faces lifts his projection 8% or more, and Tough matchup means it cuts it 8% or more. These are projections, not Vegas lines. Touchdown scorers are on the TD Props tab.
