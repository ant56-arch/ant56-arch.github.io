How the ratings work

Offense is the points a team would score against an average FBS defense, and defense the points it would allow to an average FBS offense (lower is better). Overall is the difference.

Every result is adjusted for who the team played, and both the score and how efficiently the team moved the ball count (garbage time left out). Each team starts the season from last year's rating, kept more for teams bringing back more of their production, plus roster talent, and this season's games take over as they come in.

Tested on 2022-2026 games, they picked 72% of FBS winners (the Vegas favorite: 73%). Home field is worth 4.2 points.
